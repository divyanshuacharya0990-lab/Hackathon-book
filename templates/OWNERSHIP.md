## Escalation rule
Handle own code, prompts, testing, bug fixes independently. Coordinate overlapping edits and preserve others' work. Escalate material scope, architecture, security/privacy or shared compatibility changes, destructive operations and spending outside existing authorization. Routine compatible repairs and implementation choices need no new approval. Solo developers can hold all roles. A developer may use **their own** separate prompting assistant but must supply same versioned spec and may not treat its suggestions as authoritative contract changes.
